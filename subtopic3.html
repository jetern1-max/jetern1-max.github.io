Fan Experience & Media Innovation


URL Using AR & VR To Reimagine The Fan Experience
Site Author Dhiraj Adya
Abstract The Forbes Business Council article explains how augmented reality (AR) and virtual reality (VR) are transforming sports stadiums into immersive, borderless experiences. Instead of simply watching games, fans can now engage through features like real-time data overlays, virtual perspectives from players’ viewpoints, and interactive replays that deepen emotional connection. These technologies allow fans anywhere in the world to share the same virtual environment, creating a “stadium without walls.” The article emphasizes a shift from passive viewing to active participation, where fans feel part of the game. Ultimately, AR and VR enhance storytelling, strengthen fan loyalty, and prioritize emotional connection. The future of sports lies in blending technology with human experience to create lasting, meaningful engagement beyond the game itself.


URL Sports Digitalisation
Site Author Lily Haffner, Ilan Oshri, Julia Kotlarsky
Abstract This review looks at how research in Information Systems explains the rise of digital technology in sports, focusing on different people involved like players, managers, referees, and fans. It breaks down when technology is used (before, during, and after games) and what effects it has, such as changes in behavior, thinking, and performance. The study points out some gaps, like not paying enough attention to non-players and not studying how technology affects people during live games. It also notes that most research only looks at one piece of technology and one group at a time. To improve future research, the authors suggest studying all stakeholders together, using more realistic data from real games, and combining existing theories with new digital ideas.
