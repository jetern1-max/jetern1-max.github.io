URL Application of Wearable Technologies for Player Motion
Site Author Chantelle Jean Rigozzi, Gareth A Vio, and Philip Poronnik
Abstract This systematic review looks at how wearable devices are being used to analyze player movement in racket sports like tennis, table tennis, badminton, and squash. The goal was to understand what kinds of wearable technology are used and how researchers analyze the data they collect. The authors searched several major research databases and followed strict review guidelines, narrowing down over 6,000 studies to just 15 that met all requirements. Most of the research focused on tennis and table tennis. The most common tools used were motion sensors and muscle activity sensors. These devices were mainly used to compare player skill levels, study differences in body and racket movement, and analyze how different ball spins affect motion. Overall, the review shows that wearable technology can improve training by giving athletes more detailed and personalized feedback on their performance.


URL What Is Sports Technology? Its Impact On Athlete Performance
Site Author Dr. Matthew Loux & Bryce Loux
Abstract Sports technology is the use of digital tools and devices to help athletes improve performance, training, and the overall sports experience. It includes things like wearable devices, video analysis, artificial intelligence, and data tracking systems. These tools can measure things such as speed, heart rate, movement, and distance, which helps coaches and athletes understand performance better and reduce the risk of injury. Sports technology is also used to improve coaching decisions, game strategies, and officiating accuracy. It can even make watching sports more exciting for fans through new viewing experiences and statistics. Many sports teams at all levels use this technology today. However, there are some concerns, such as privacy issues and not all teams having equal access to advanced tools. Overall, sports technology is changing how athletes train, compete, and how fans experience sports.
